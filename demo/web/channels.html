Two screens to know

🛒 Order Management System (OMS) — where orders happen
This page — localhost:8902/channels
Pick a channel tab (DTC / Amazon / Etsy / Walmart) → type a quantity → Place order. Order fulfillment happens on IMS.
📋 Inventory management System (IMS) — where the agent reports
The ops dashboard — localhost:8902/ops
The Orders tab is where you Fulfill orders. The Smart Allocation tab shows what the agent decided — green Auto-applied cards it wrote itself, amber Needs approval cards waiting for a human.

Scenario A — Needs approval

  1. Stay on OMS (this page).
  2. Click the Amazon tab. On the MUG-001 row, type 30 in the number box.
  3. Click Place order.
  4. Open IMS (/ops) and click the Orders tab. Find the new MUG-001 order in Open orders and click ✔ Fulfill All.
  5. Wait about 1–2 minutes — the agent runs automatically after fulfillment.
  6. Click the Smart Allocation tab (still on IMS) → the amber Needs approval card would show.
  7. Click ✔ Approve (green) or ✘ Deny (red) on the card to finish.
Why it asks: the order drains most of MUG-001’s stock, so the agent’s fix moves more than 25% of a channel’s units (or empties a channel) — big moves always need a human yes/no.

Scenario B — Auto-applied

  1. Stay on OMS (this page).
  2. Click the DTC tab. On LAMP-002, type 20 → Click Place order.
  3. Click the Amazon tab. On LAMP-002, type 20 → Click Place order.
  4. Open IMS (/ops) and click the Orders tab.
  5. Click ✔ Fulfill All on both new LAMP-002 orders.
  6. Wait about 1–2 minutes — the agent runs automatically after fulfillment.
  7. Click the Smart Allocation tab → the green Auto-applied card shows units moved to the higher-margin channel and the dollars saved.
Why it just acts: DTC earns the most per LAMP-002 sale, so refilling it from slower channels is a clear win. No channel emptied and no move over 25% → the agent applies it without asking.

Good to know

• The agent only wakes up after a fulfillment, not when an order is placed — don’t skip Fulfill.
• On IMS, the Orders tab shows a red dot with the open-order count; Smart Allocation shows one with pending approvals.
• No card after ~2 minutes? Open IMS and press ▶ Run allocation cycle once.
• Want a clean slate? ↻ Reset demo data (top right, this page) restores stock, orders and allocations.
